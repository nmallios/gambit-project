About the project

Learning that strengthens agency.

GAMBIT is an Erasmus+ project that uses chess as an accessible, structured and engaging learning tool for incarcerated adult learners in Greece and Lithuania.

Through lessons, collaborative challenges, educator training and a final international tournament, the project supports problem-solving, decision-making, teamwork, communication and fair play. Its approach is designed for correctional settings, where meaningful educational opportunities can promote personal development and social inclusion.

Our objectives

Four moves towards meaningful learning

01

Think strategically

Develop problem-solving, critical thinking and decision-making through structured chess learning.

02

Learn together

Strengthen communication, teamwork, mutual respect and fair play through collaborative activities.

03

Empower educators

Provide prison educators and facilitators with adaptable, practical and reusable teaching materials.

04

Open new pathways

Support motivation, confidence and participation as foundations for personal growth and reintegration.

Project activities

From toolkit to tournament

Five connected activities turn an educational idea into a practical, sustainable resource for correctional education.

  1. 01

    Project Management & Implementation

    Coordination, quality assurance, communication, risk management and partner collaboration throughout the project.

    Sep 2026–Nov 2027
  2. 02

    The GAMBIT Toolkit

    A trilingual package of chess lessons, quizzes, collaborative activities, multimedia resources and a guide for educators.

    Oct 2026–Jun 2027
  3. 03

    Learning to Teach Chess in Prison

    A Lithuania-based training workshop for adult educators and chess facilitators working in correctional settings.

    Jul 2027
  4. 04

    Strategic Teamwork: Chess Lessons & Challenges

    Weekly chess-based learning sessions and feedback cycles for participating adult learners in both countries.

    Jul–Oct 2027
  5. 05

    Chess Tournament & Dissemination

    An online team tournament and national dissemination events to share results and encourage wider adoption.

    Nov 2027
